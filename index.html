Our story

Grassroots work that didn't start yesterday.

Our organisation has been actively involved in grassroots mobilisation since 2015, with an established network and a lasting commitment to the party.

2015 & 2019

We supported the presidential campaigns of former President Muhammadu Buhari and former Vice President Professor Yemi Osinbajo in the 2015 and 2019 general elections.

2022 – 2023

We adapted our campaign identity to support the APC presidential ticket of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu and Vice President Kashim Shettima in the 2023 general elections, keeping our grassroots network and our commitment to the party.

2027 and beyond

We are mobilising again for Asiwaju/Shettima, and building a permanent line between citizens and government that outlasts every election.

Mission & Vision

Where the people and the government meet.

Our Mission

To mobilise Nigerians at the grassroots in defence of democracy and good governance, from the state down to the local government, ward and polling unit across the 36 states and the FCT, and to serve as a trusted bridge between citizens and government: carrying the concerns, needs and ideas of the people upward, and bringing feedback and action back down, during elections and long after them.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every citizen, in every ward and community, has a working line to those who govern; where leaders listen, respond and deliver; and where participation in democracy does not end when the votes are counted.

The bridge in action

From the street corner to the seat of power, and back.

After the elections, the work continues. This is how the platform keeps citizens and government connected.

Speak

Members and communities raise their needs, complaints and ideas through ward and local structures.

Gather

Concerns are documented, organised and prioritised so they reach government clearly and credibly.

Deliver

We carry the people's message to the relevant offices and follow it up respectfully and persistently.

Report back

Responses and action from government are brought back to the community, closing the loop.
