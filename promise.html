The Promise

A candid promise to the people.

A message from the National Coordinator, Elder Comr. Osumiri Paul Efe, LLB Hons, MSA.

Our pledge
"10 million votes will be mobilised from the 36 states of the Federation and the FCT for Asiwaju/Shettima come 2027. This is our candid promise to you as your Support Group."

The National Coordinator's message sets out why the platform exists, why grassroots support groups matter, and what members will do between now and election day. It is summarised below.

Why Asiwaju Tinubu

A leader who stays relevant.

In the Coordinator's words, supporters call President Bola Ahmed Tinubu "The Game Changer" and the "Asiwaju of Nigeria's Democracy", and others regard him as a master strategist. He served two terms as Governor of Lagos State between 1999 and 2007.

The Coordinator points to his continued relevance in national politics, long after leaving the governorship, as the reason for the platform's choice. The message closes with the rallying line: "After Tinubu, Tinubu again. 2027 is non-negotiable."

Why grassroots matter

Support groups are the eyes and voice of the people.

The Coordinator describes support groups as a major tool in any election: the grassroots mobilisers, the foot soldiers, and the eyes and voice of a candidate and the party. They are, he says, the real electorate.

He states that the platform can deliver its votes for Asiwaju/Shettima and all APC candidates across Nigeria, provided the necessary facilities and logistics are in place, and that the President has always recognised the usefulness of support groups.

The platform's membership is built on valid, accredited members with verifiable, eligible voters, from the state to the local government and ward, down to the polling unit, across the 36 states and the FCT.

Our agenda

The twelve-point agenda, in six commitments.

Win in 2027

Ensure victory for Asiwaju/Shettima and all APC candidates, and deliver votes in large numbers at every level.

Vote, and protect the vote

Vote, make every vote count, stand to protect it, and work for free and fair elections.

Rallies in every state

Hold local-government-to-local-government campaign rallies across the 36 states and the FCT.

Reach every door

Sensitisation street to street and door to door, in cities, villages and creeks, urban and rural.

A working structure

State, LGA, ward and unit coordinators who supervise well, with every member turning out in numbers on election day.

Promote Renewed Hope

Make the candidate, the party and the Renewed Hope agenda widely accepted, and distribute campaign materials: uniforms, T-shirts, caps, posters and flyers.

Signed
Elder Comr. Osumiri Paul Efe, LLB Hons, MSA
National Coordinator, APC Grassroots Mobilization, Asiwaju/Shettima 2027
